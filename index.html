SemantEq: Faithful Bidirectional Equivalence of Formal Statements
Xinrui Li, Pawan Sasanka Ammanamanchi, Siddharth Bhat, Rianna Jamdar, Yongxi Lin, Stella Biderman
arXiv preprint, 2026 · under review at ICLR 2027
arXiv / code

Logical equivalence is too permissive a test of whether a formal statement faithfully matches its reference, because a strong prover can connect any two provable statements through P ↔ True ↔ Q. SemantEq is a finer relation under which each statement must turn into the other by meaning-preserving steps alone, such as logical rearrangement and rewriting with the library's own equations, and a proof is judged by the term Lean checks rather than by its tactic script. SemantEq-Eval searches for such chains with no model and reaches 100% precision on ProofNetVerif. SemantEq-ProofAudit holds proofs written by a language model to the same standard, accepting none of the non-equivalent pairs while raising recall from 65% to 81%.

ProofGym: Unifying LLM-Based Theorem Proving Across Formal Systems
Xinrui Li, Wenjie Ma, Hangrui Bi, Zhaoyu Li, Xujie Si, Kaiyu Yang
NeurIPS 2025 Workshop on Mathematical Reasoning and AI (MATH-AI), 2025
OpenReview

Infrastructure for LLM-based theorem proving that spans Lean, Coq, and Isabelle, so that the same prover, data pipeline, and evaluation can be used across proof assistants instead of being rebuilt for each one.
